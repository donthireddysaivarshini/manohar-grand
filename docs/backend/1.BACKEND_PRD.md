# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Manohar Grand Hotel Backend

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
Manohar Grand is a premier hospitality destination in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, located within 1-minute walking distance of JNTU Metro Station and Nexus Forum Mall. This document defines the backend product requirements for the hotel's centralized, authoritative Direct Booking Platform.

The backend serves as the single source of truth for:
- Room inventory & calendar availability derived strictly from active physical rooms
- Authoritative reservation lifecycle & configurable hold management (default: 15 minutes)
- Centralized rule-based dynamic pricing & immutable financial snapshots
- 50% advance online payment orchestration via Razorpay with 50% due at check-in
- Unified multi-channel inventory synchronization (online direct booking, front-desk walk-in, phone, WhatsApp/direct, corporate)
- Configurable hotel policy & dynamic CMS engine
- Secure role-based staff/admin operations (Owner/SuperAdmin, Manager, Receptionist) and Google OAuth customer authentication

## 3. Stakeholder Personas & Core Journeys

### 3.1 Customer (Direct Guest)
- **Search & Discovery**: Searches stay dates (11:00 AM check-in / check-out), guests, and room count.
- **Authoritative Availability**: Views real-time category bookability derived from active physical rooms.
- **Room Selection**: Selects category (e.g. AC Room × 1). Physical room number is assigned later by reception.
- **50% Advance Checkout**: Pays 50% advance online via Razorpay to confirm booking; 50% balance due at check-in.
- **Authentication**: Authenticates seamlessly using "Continue with Google" (Google OAuth via standard `django-allauth`).
- **Confirmation & Account**: Receives unique booking reference code (`MG-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`). Public lookup requires secure `access_token`; full booking history accessible via authenticated account.

### 3.2 Hotel Receptionist (Front Desk Staff)
- **Dashboard Overview**: Views today's arrivals, departures, live occupied rooms, and vacant physical inventory.
- **Offline Walk-In / Phone / WhatsApp Booking**: Creates instant offline bookings, deducting from the same unified inventory.
- **Physical Room Assignment**: Assigns specific physical room numbers (e.g., Room 105) to confirmed category reservations.
- **Balance Payment & Check-In**: Collects remaining 50% balance (cash/POS/UPI) and marks guest arrival.
- **ID Verification & Aadhaar Upload**: Verifies physical ID and uploads document copy into secure storage.

### 3.3 Hotel Owner & Manager (Administrator)
- **Inventory & Rate Management**: Updates base room rates (AC ₹1,599 / Non-AC ₹1,299), extra guest charges (₹350 adult / ₹300 child), and tax rules (GST 5%) via Admin Panel without code changes.
- **Physical Room Management**: Adds, activates/deactivates physical rooms, or sets maintenance blocks.
- **Overbooking Override (Owner Only)**: Explicitly creates emergency/VIP overbookings with mandatory audit trail.
- **Reports & Audit Trail**: Exports occupancy, revenue, GST liability, and staff activity logs.
